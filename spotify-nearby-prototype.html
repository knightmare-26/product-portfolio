Spotify "Nearby": live prototype

A Spotify-style Nearby screen powered by the real recommendation logic from the case study, running on a small sample catalogue. Each track gets a score of personal taste × local context. Then the guardrail rules (cold-start fallback, artist cap, long-tail reserve, weekly rotation) shape the final mix. Change any control and the phone re-ranks live. The breakdown underneath shows exactly why each track landed where it did.
