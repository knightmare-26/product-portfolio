"Nearby" — Context-Aware Local Music Discovery

Mocked up inside Spotify's real interface language — the Home shelf structure, shortcut grid, mini-player, and tab bar — so "Nearby" reads as a native extension rather than a separate app. Four screens: the Home entry point, two contrasting cities (Barcelona and Nashville), and the location-permission screen.
